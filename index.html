About the Challenge

India's National Factory Automation Competition

The Mitsubishi Electric Cup — AutomationX Innovation Challenge 2027 — brings together undergraduate and postgraduate engineering students to build real-world automation solutions with industry-grade hardware, hosted by CVR College of Engineering, Hyderabad.

Innovate & Solve

Sharpen engineering application skills, innovation, teamwork and problem-solving on real manufacturing and energy challenges.

Industry Exposure

Build Your Career

Become future-ready talent for AI-enabled, intelligent, sustainable and connected smart factory ecosystems.

Your Host

Mitsubishi Electric FA Authorised Training Center

A decade-long, industry-integrated initiative at CVR College of Engineering dedicated to automation skill development, practical training and innovative product design.

Established in February 2015 in collaboration with Mitsubishi Electric India Pvt. Ltd., the Factory Automation Lab was built with ₹80 lakhs of sponsored equipment to give students and industry professionals hands-on exposure to advanced industrial automation.

Recognised as a Mitsubishi Electric Authorised Training Center (ATC), the lab is one of only 10 ATCs in India — and the only one in Telangana and Andhra Pradesh — making CVR a hub for industrial automation training and industry–academia engagement.

Training Modules & Contents

L1 · PLC

MELSEC iQ-F PLC

Introduction to iQ-F series, I/O addressing, programming with GX Works3, applied instructions, timers, counters, analog interface, function blocks & libraries.

L1 · HMI

GOT2000

GOT2000 hardware, screen design with GT Designer3, alarms, graphs, comments, recipes, ladder monitoring & editing, program diagnosis.

L1 · Network

CC-Link & CC-Link IE Field

Network addressing & connections, master–remote and CPU–CPU interface, troubleshooting, hardware tests, IE Field topology and remote I/O stations.

L1 · Drives

VFD A800

Inverter basics, A800/E800 series, V/F control, jogging, parameter setting, multi-speed operation and troubleshooting.

L2 · PLC

MELSEC iQ-R PLC

iQ-R vs Q series, compatibility in existing lines, library functions, applied instructions, data labels, analog interface, maintenance & security functions.

L2 · HMI

GOT2000 Advanced

Program diagnosis & backup via GOT, one-touch jump, communication options, data logging and graphs.

L2 · Network

EtherNet/IP & Third-Party

EtherNet/IP and third-party communication — DeviceNet, Profibus, Modbus TCP.

L2 · Servo

Servo MR-J4-GF

Mitsubishi servo range, CC-Link IE Field based controller & amplifier, positioning with QD77GF, M-codes and the Simple Motion configurator.

L3 · Safety

Safety PLC

Safety CPU features, iQ-R safety I/O, wiring of safety PLC & remote I/O, CC-Link IE Field Network Safety, project creation and station settings.
